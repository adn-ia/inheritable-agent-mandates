What to look at. Every lease in this contract has now lapsed. These agents were created on 8 September with short leases, and they died on their own clocks, with nobody intervening — which is the death condition the standard requires: reachable without the child's cooperation. A dead child cannot be asked to agree, so the trigger must not need it.

Agent 2 shows already reclaimed: yes — its forty ether went back to agent 1, whose unallocated budget is whole again. Agents 4 and 6 are dead and still reclaimable.

The case that cannot be shown here, because it depends on a moment that has passed, is a live child under a frozen parent: inactive, yet not dead, and its budget not yours to take. It was sent on chain so it could be cited rather than described — that transaction fails with child not dead.
